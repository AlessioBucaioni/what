# What Will Software Architects Really Do? Revisiting the Architect’s Role in the Age of Generative AI

Replication package for the paper. Open index.html directly or serve this directory with a static HTTP server. The site needs no installation, account or backend and loads no remote fonts, assets or trackers.

## What is included

The study comprises 13 interview sessions with 15 participants. The package contains 12 de-identified transcript text copies, 25 approved codes, seven final themes, a cross-cutting qualifier, a peripheral finding and nine interpretive relationships. It also includes the RQ mapping and its rationales, methodology, interview guide and all 276 evidence IDs: 259 available quotations and 17 restricted records.

I3 did not agree to public sharing. The analysis includes that interview, but its transcript, quotations, source paragraphs and prompt text are withheld. Its evidence IDs and analytical links remain visible so readers can see where source material is unavailable.

## How to read the package

Start with a theme, open its connected codes and follow an evidence ID to the source paragraph. Transcript paragraphs link back to the evidence register. Search applies to the current analysis view. Hash-based links work on static hosting; JSON, CSV and text exports can be read without the interface.

The transcript copies preserve paragraph order, recorded timestamps and automatic-transcription artifacts, except for marked identifying redactions. They are text exports rather than copies of the Word files. A quotation is flagged when identifying text has been redacted. Short labels in the navigation do not replace the full approved theme names.

## Method and interpretation

The methodology account follows the current manuscript: a qualitative interview study with inductive codebook thematic analysis. It describes recruitment, interview refinements, researcher review of model suggestions and the limits of the analysis. Interview questions are shortened as presented in the manuscript. Current practice, interpretation, expected futures and desired futures should be read separately.

GPT-6 Astra assisted analytic suggestions and candidate-theme development; researchers checked the source material and approved the codes and themes. AI output is not an additional empirical source or independent validation. AI assistance was also used to prepare this interface and the exports.

## Sharing and identification

Names, identified organizations, selected locations, identifying background details and recording metadata have been removed or generalized. Context may still allow recognition; de-identification does not guarantee anonymity. Original Word files, internal research histories, private review notes and local paths are not included.

## Files

- index.html and assets/: website.
- data/: JSON and CSV exports; package.js allows offline use.
- transcripts/: 12 marked sharing copies in UTF-8 text.
- downloads/: methodology, interview guide, readable analysis and a materials ZIP.
- MANIFEST.json: file sizes and SHA-256 hashes, excluding the manifest itself and ZIP bundles.

## Hosting and reuse

Only this site directory belongs in the anonymous repository. Do not upload its parent directory or the original research workspace. Hosting is configured separately; this local build has not been published.

No blanket license is assigned to the interview material. Public access does not override consent or third-party rights. The authors should settle citation and licensing information before publication.
